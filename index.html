BioVerse

Bioverse is a student-led biotechnology club at the European University of Valencia. We are dedicated to deepening understanding of biotechnological applications through both practical and theoretical learning. Our club facilitates connections between students and leading figures in the industry and academia, enabling them to gain firsthand insights and enhance their skills. We aim to inspire innovation and ethical practices in biotechnology, equipping our members to make significant contributions to global challenges.
